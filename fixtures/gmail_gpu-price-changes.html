March 2026 Update
Scaling DeepInfra for the Next Wave of AI
It's been a massive year for AI, and we've loved watching your projects move into high gear. As demand for high-performance inference continues to surge, we're making some adjustments to our private GPU pricing starting March 17, 2026 — so we can keep our fleet growing and stay ready for whatever comes next.
Due to rising costs of high-performance memory and storage components, we're updating pricing on select private GPUs. Here's a full breakdown:

Your per-token pricing isn't changing. We want to be upfront about that. Pricing on all of our hosted models stays exactly the same; these updates only affect private GPU rentals.

These changes reflect our continued investments in expanding GPU clusters, improving inference throughput and latency, bringing next-generation hardware like the B300 online, and maintaining reliable global infrastructure.
What this means for you
Even with these adjustments, DeepInfra remains one of the most cost-efficient inference platforms available. Our B300s are priced at the best rates in the market, and we're committed to keeping it that way.

Many of our customers continue to cut costs further through batching, optimized pipelines, and shifting workloads to newer architectures.
We're incredibly grateful for the community that's built on DeepInfra — from solo open-source developers to large production teams processing trillions of tokens. You're the reason we keep pushing.
