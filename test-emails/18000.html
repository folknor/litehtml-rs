Before we get started, I’d like to take a minute to talk about the topic…of emails. 

I think there has been some confusion out there about my emails as Secretary of State, so I want to talk about it directly.

When I was in the Senate, I used a Blackberry for both work and personal emails. When I got to work as Secretary,  I wanted to do the same thing.  So I opted to use my personal email account -  which was permitted by the department -  because I thought it would be easier to carry just one device for my work and for my personal emails, instead of two.  

A lot of you know, I’m not exactly a technological whiz.

Looking back, it would have been better if I had simply used a second email account and carried a second phone, but at the time, this certainly didn’t seem like an issue.

I sent my work emails to government employees at their government addresses, which meant they were immediately preserved on the system at the State Department.  

After I left office, when the Department asked  for all emails from former secretaries of state sent from their personal accounts, I responded  right away and we have turned over all of my work related emails,  which total roughly 55,000 printed pages. 

I have also asked that the State Department make all of these emails public so everyone can see them for themselves.
