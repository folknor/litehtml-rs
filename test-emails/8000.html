DRAFT launch statement
PROGRESSIVE MEDIA

Progressive Media will serve as a communications and messaging nerve
center for the progressive movement. We will create a robust echo
chamber with messaging that spans from independent groups, to
progressive partisans, to grassroots organizers, to the netroots. We
will align messaging across broadcast and cable TV, radio, print,
Internet, single-issue and advocacy organizations, progressive media,
surrogates and new media.  Through Progressive Media, during the 2008
election campaign we will focus on showing the public where John
McCain stands on key issues facing America and the world. We seek to
become a part of the permanent progressive infrastructure to last
beyond the 2008 election. Breaking through the clutter of the modern
media environment with a progressive message requires repetition of
common themes from different sources organized by a central
apparatus--that is the organizing task of Progressive Media.
