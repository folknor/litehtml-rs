Gullinbursti Dividend Portfolio – February 2026 Update

Strongest month since May – portfolio remains firmly ahead of the benchmark

Sigbjørn Hovda
Mar 2
 
READ IN APP
 

February saw a powerful continuation of the rally that began late last year. The OSEBX climbed +7.47%, supported by strength in energy and shipping.

Gullinbursti advanced +5.92% during the month, marking its strongest monthly performance since May last year. While this was slightly below the index return, the portfolio maintains a clear lead since inception in late April 2025.

Total return now stands at +45.83%, approximately 14 percentage points ahead of the OSEBX over the same period.

As always, full transparency is provided through the shared Google Sheet tracking every trade, dividend, invested capital and cash position.

💸 Dividends

February contributed modest income:

  • +0.51% dividend return during the month (DNO and Panoro Energy)

  • +8.64% cumulative dividend contribution since inception

Dividends continue to represent a structural component of total return, reinforcing the portfolio’s income-focused profile even in months where capital gains dominate.

📊 Portfolio Activity

February included two notable portfolio adjustments:

  • Sold: PetroNor at NOK 11.00 (ahead of Q4)
    We sold PetroNor the day before Q4. While the report confirmed improving production and a strong cash position, legal uncertainty continues to influence dividend visibility. The exit reflected risk management and portfolio balance rather than operational concerns.

  • Sold: Elmera ahead of Q4
    The position was exited following a strong re-rating and ahead of earnings. Subsequent share price weakness of more than 20% underscores the value of reducing exposure when short-term risk increases relative to upside.

Following these adjustments, the portfolio now holds approximately 34% in cash, providing significant flexibility. We are currently evaluating opportunities and expect to add at least one new position shortly.

Key Contributors

Panoro Energy (PEN.OL)

Panoro was the strongest performer in February, rising 21.4% during the month. The move reflects market reassessment following the transformational operational step change recently outlined, including higher production capacity and improved asset diversification. Increased production visibility, stronger free cash flow potential and a clearer medium-term dividend framework continue to support the longer-term case, even as the market had previously priced in elevated uncertainty around 2026 distributions.
